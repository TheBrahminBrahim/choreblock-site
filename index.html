How it works

Three steps between you and your apps — and a tidier home every single day.

1 Your baseline

Set your chores

Pick your chores and snap a photo of each spot looking tidy. That photo becomes your ‘clean’ baseline.

2

When a chore is due, apps are locked

Each chore has its own rhythm — dishes daily, the dog walk twice a day. The moment one is due, your chosen apps lock automatically.

3
Baseline
Just now

Prove it with a photo

Do the chore, snap a live photo, and AI compares it to your baseline. Pass the check and your apps unlock until the next chore comes due.

Chores that actually count

Every chore is something a single photo can honestly verify — visible tidiness, not vague promises.

Kitchen sink Dishes done, sink empty
Made bed Covers up, pillows set
Bathroom counter Clutter cleared
Empty dishwasher Racks cleared out
Walk the dog Dog + outdoors = pass

Your photos are never stored

Every photo is checked and immediately discarded — never saved, never logged, never used to train anything. Capture is camera-only, so old photos from your library can't be used to cheat.
