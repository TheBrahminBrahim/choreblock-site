2. What ChoreBlock is (and isn't)

ChoreBlock uses Apple's Screen Time framework to lock apps you select, on a schedule you set, and unlocks them when a photo of a finished chore passes an automated check. A few things to know:

  • It's self-imposed. You can pause any chore, change the schedule, unlock your apps early, remove the Screen Time permission, or delete the app at any time. ChoreBlock is a nudge, not a wall.
  • The lock is only as strong as Apple's Screen Time. Locking depends on iOS features we don't control. An iOS update, a Screen Time setting, a system restriction, or a bug can stop a lock from applying or lift it early. We don't guarantee that any app will be locked, or unlocked, at any particular time.
  • Photo checks are automated and can be wrong. A computer model judges each photo and may pass a chore that isn't done or fail one that is. When a check fails, you can try again, and after repeated misses you can mark the chore done yourself. Nobody at Scarabyte reviews your photos.
  • Don't lock anything you might urgently need. You're responsible for choosing which apps to block. Don't block apps you rely on for safety, health, work, or emergencies.
