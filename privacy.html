CHOREBLOCK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

ChoreBlock is built by Scarabyte. The app locks distracting apps until you complete a household chore, verified by a live photo. This policy explains what data the app touches and — more importantly — what it never keeps.

The short version: with your permission, your chore photos go to Google's AI to be checked. We don't keep them; Google may log them for up to 55 days to catch abuse. Your app-usage settings never leave your phone, and we don't have user accounts, ads, or data sales. Ever.

Chore photos and AI

The app asks your permission before it sends your first photo. When you check a chore, the photo you just took and your reference photo for that chore are sent over an encrypted connection to our verification service, hosted by Supabase. It passes them to Google's Gemini AI, which compares them and returns a pass or fail result. Your name and account aren't sent with them, but please keep people and private papers out of shot. When you set up a reference photo, it goes through the same check on its own.

What we do with them: our service doesn't save or log your photos. It drops them as soon as the check finishes, the check photo is deleted from your phone, and nobody at Scarabyte sees them. Your reference photos are saved only on your phone, and a copy is sent with each check.

What Google does with them: we use Google's paid Gemini service, so Google doesn't use your photos to improve its products. Under its terms, Google logs what is sent for a limited time to detect abuse and meet legal requirements. Its usage policy currently gives this as 55 days, and authorized Google staff may review anything its systems flag. This data may be held temporarily in any country where Google has facilities. We can't delete Google's copy, because nothing links a photo to you.

You can say no. Without permission no photos are sent, but chores can't be checked. To withdraw permission later, use Delete All My Data in Settings, which also removes your chores from the app.

Capture is camera-only: the app has no access to your photo library.

To measure and improve how accurate the check is, we keep an anonymous record of each check's result — which chore, whether it passed, how sure the check was, a multiple-choice reason it didn't pass, how long it took, the hour of day, which app version ran it and whether it was a test build, and a random code that groups the retries within one visit to the camera (it is made up fresh each time, is not saved on your phone, and cannot be traced to you) — plus a weekly count of how many checks the app made. The record never includes the photo, the written feedback you see, your IP address, or anything that identifies you or your device. It is stored in our own database, hosted by Supabase.

Screen Time data

ChoreBlock uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to lock and unlock the apps you choose. Apple's design keeps this data private by architecture: the list of apps you block is held in opaque tokens that ChoreBlock cannot read and that never leave your device. We cannot see which apps you block or how you use your phone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and purchase receipts — never your name, email, or photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Purchase and marketing measurement

To understand which of our marketing (creator videos, promo codes and ads) leads to downloads and subscriptions, we collect a small amount of anonymous purchase data:

  • Purchase events from RevenueCat. When you start a trial, subscribe, renew, cancel, switch plans or get a refund, RevenueCat sends us a record of that event: an anonymous app-generated user ID, the product, price and currency, your country, the dates, and any promo code or campaign it was linked to. We store these records in our own database, hosted by Supabase, to count trials, customers and revenue.

This data never includes your name, email, photos or Screen Time data. We do not use your advertising identifier (IDFA), do not ask to track you, do not combine this data with data from other companies to profile you, and do not use it to show you ads. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Crash reports

We use Sentry to collect crash reports and performance data so we can fix bugs. These reports contain technical details about the crash: device model, OS version, battery and memory state, what the app was doing, a random code for your install of the app, and your approximate country. They never include your photos, name or email. See Sentry's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts — nothing to sign up for, nothing to breach
  • No ads and no third-party ad tracking
  • No selling of personal data, and no sharing except with the service providers named on this page to run the app
  • No location tracking, step counting, or motion sensors
  • No photo storage of any kind on our servers (see "Chore photos and AI" for Google's temporary log)

Data retention & deletion

We don't keep chore photos after the check, and your settings and reference photos live only on your device, so deleting the app deletes the data we hold about you there. Google's temporary log is described under "Chore photos and AI". There is no user profile on our servers. The anonymous purchase records described under "Purchase and marketing measurement" contain nothing that identifies you, and we keep them only as long as we need them for business and accounting records. Anonymous check results are deleted automatically after 90 days; only monthly totals are kept.

Children

ChoreBlock is designed for adults managing their own screen time and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. Material changes will be called out in the app's release notes.
